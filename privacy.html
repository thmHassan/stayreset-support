StayReset

Privacy policy

Updated 17 October 2026

Summary: StayReset has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated server. Your checklists, notes and chosen photos are stored on your device. You control when a PDF report is shared.

Information handled

The app stores property names, checklist steps and status, turnover dates, notes and the photos you choose to add. It creates a PDF report from those details when you request one.

Storage and sharing

Data is kept in the app's storage on your device. Android backup is disabled in this build. On iOS, your device's backup settings may include app data. When you use Share PDF, the PDF is handed to the app or service you choose; that destination handles it under its own privacy terms. StayReset does not send your content to the developer.

Camera and photo access

The app requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to attach a photo to a checklist step. The selected photo is copied into app storage. It is included in the PDF if you create and share a report.

Data removal

You can delete a property through its options menu, which removes its checklists, turnover history and attached photos from app storage. Replacing a photo removes the previous app copy. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data, subject to any device backups you control. Copies you have shared, saved outside the app or backed up must be removed separately.

Children

StayReset is a work tool for adult hosts and cleaners and is not directed at children.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the public support issue tracker. A direct support email will be added before store submission.
